# OpenSpec instructions

- Keep `proposal.md`, `design.md`, `tasks.md`, and delta specifications
  consistent.
- Keep Jira and Figma responses under `context/intake/`.
- Keep discovered repository and dependency data under `context/discovery/`.
- Keep repository routing under `context/worksets/`.
- Keep atomic task claims under `context/claims/`.
- Keep machine task state under `context/task-status/`.
- Do not use folder names as dependency decisions.
- Do not mark a dependent task eligible until its declared prerequisite state
  is satisfied.
